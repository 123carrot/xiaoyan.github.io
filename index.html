About me

Hi, I’m Xiaoyan! I am a master’s student in Data Science and Engineering at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Wei Chen and Prof. Wei Zhang at ZJUVAI, State Key Laboratory of CAD&CG. I also work closely with Prof. Yifang Wang.

My research focuses on how humans and AI shape each other through sustained interaction, how these changes accumulate over time, and how they transform the collective capacities of human–AI systems to understand, reason, and act. I combine AI methods with HCI and visualization techniques to investigate these processes and design interactive systems that account for both how humans shape AI and how AI influences human thinking, learning, and behavior beyond the immediate task.

I have also worked on digital humanities, including artwork analysis and benchmarks for visual reasoning and cultural understanding.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Chongqing University.

Interests: Multimodal Large Language Model Post-Training · Agents · Human–AI Interaction · Visualization

Multimodal post-training · Reward design · GRPO
